# Electron-Molecule Scattering

## Overview

This project investigates low-energy electron scattering from molecules using
computational quantum chemistry and scattering theory.

The goal is to calculate and analyze electron-scattering cross sections and
identify important features such as Ramsauer-Townsend minima and resonance
peaks.

## My Work

I investigated electron-molecule scattering using two computational approaches,
UKRmol+ and ASTRA.

The project involved setting up molecular electronic structure calculations,
constructing scattering calculations, running simulations on a high-performance
computing cluster, analyzing the resulting cross sections, and comparing the
calculated results with values reported in the literature.

## Computational Methods

### UKRmol+

UKRmol+ was used to perform electron-molecule scattering calculations using
a close-coupling approach.

### ASTRA

ASTRA was used as a second computational approach for calculating
electron-molecule scattering observables.

Comparing the two approaches allowed me to investigate how different
computational methods and model parameters affect the calculated scattering
results.

## Software & Tools

- UKRmol+
- ASTRA
- Psi4

## Results

The calculations were used to obtain electron-scattering cross sections as a
function of incident electron energy.

The resulting cross sections showed important scattering features, including
a Ramsauer-Townsend minimum and resonance structures.
