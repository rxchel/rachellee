Ab Initio Electron-Molecule Scattering Calculations for Methane Using UKRMol+ and ASTRA

Dr. Thomas C. Allison and Dr. Siddhartha Chattopadhyay · National Institute of Standards and Technology

Computational investigation of low-energy electron scattering from methane using UKRmol+ and ASTRA. The project explores scattering cross sections, Ramsauer-Townsend minima, and resonance features for plasma modeling relevant to semiconductor manufacturing and chip etching.

Tools: UKRmol+, ASTRA, Psi4, Python, Linux
