# Hello, I'm Rachel Lee.

### Chemical Engineering · Computational Science · Materials Research

I am a chemical engineering undergraduate at [**The Cooper Union**](https://cooper.edu/admissions/facts) 
interested in using computational modeling, scientific programming, and experimental 
methods to understand chemical and materials systems, particularly semiconductor materials, 
molecular systems, and energy-related materials.

My experience spans both industry and academic research. I have worked with technology startups, research groups, and large financial institutions, where I have developed skills in **data analysis, programming, communication, problem-solving, collaboration, and project ownership**. These experiences have strengthened my ability to approach technical problems from both a scientific and practical perspective.

My research experience includes **electrochemical synthesis, nanomaterial growth, machine learning, molecular dynamics, computational materials science, and electron–molecule scattering**. Through these projects, I have developed experience with scientific computing, computational modeling, data processing, and analyzing complex chemical and materials systems.

I enjoy working at the intersection of chemistry, materials science, computation, and engineering, and I am particularly interested in applying computational and quantitative approaches to real-world problems in advanced materials and technology.
