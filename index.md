# Hello, I'm Rachel Lee.

### Chemical Engineering · Computational Science · Materials Research

I am a chemical engineering undergraduate at [**The Cooper Union**](https://cooper.edu/admissions/facts), interested
in using computational modeling, scientific programming, and experimental
methods to understand chemical and materials systems, particularly semiconductor materials, molecular systems, and energy-related materials.

## Research Experience

### Chemical Engineering NSF-REU Researcher
**Dr. Neeraj Rai's Laboratory · Mississippi State University**  
*May 2025 – August 2025*

I investigated the defect behavior and electronic properties of bulk
formamidinium lead bromide perovskites for semiconductor applications.

I trained machine-learning models on **ab initio molecular dynamics** data to
predict the behavior of bulk crystals containing point defects. I developed
an iterative workflow for model training, performance analysis, data
collection, and retraining to reduce prediction error.

This work resulted in a poster presentation at the **2025 Undergraduate
Research Symposium**.

[View Project →](./projects/perovskites/)

---

### Chemistry NSF-REU Researcher
**Dr. Stephen Maldonado's Laboratory · University of Michigan**  
*May 2024 – August 2024*

I studied germanium nanowire growth using an **electrochemical
liquid-liquid-solid (ec-LLS) crystal growth mechanism**, with the broader goal
of understanding materials systems relevant to battery energy storage.

I also developed procedures for fabricating micropipettes, nanopipettes,
microelectrodes, and ultramicroelectrodes to improve experimental efficiency.

This work resulted in poster presentations at the **2024 Undergraduate
Research and Networking at UMich Symposium** and the **2024 AIChE ACS Poster
Competition**.

## Selected Projects

### Electron-Molecule Scattering

Computational investigation of low-energy electron scattering from molecules
using **UKRmol+ and ASTRA**, including quantum chemistry calculations,
high-performance computing, numerical analysis, and comparison with literature
results.

[View Project →](./projects/electron-scattering/)

### Defect Behavior in Halide Perovskites

Machine-learning investigation of defective formamidinium lead bromide
perovskites using data from **ab initio molecular dynamics** simulations.

[View Project →](./projects/perovskites/)

### Germanium Nanowire Growth

Experimental investigation of germanium nanowire growth and deposition using
an electrochemical liquid-liquid-solid crystal growth mechanism.

### Socioeconomic Disaster Risk Modeling

Data science and predictive modeling work contributing to the Decision Engine
for Socioeconomic Disaster Risk (DESDR) at Columbia University's Climate
School International Research Institute.
