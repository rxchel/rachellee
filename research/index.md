# Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of computational chemistry,
materials science, chemical engineering, and scientific computing.

## PFAS in Plasma Processing

Computational investigation of PFAS molecules in plasma environments relevant
to semiconductor manufacturing.

## Halide Perovskites

Computational investigation of defect behavior in halide perovskite materials
for optoelectronic applications.

## Charged Particle Systems

Computational investigation of structure formation in charged particle systems.

## Electron-Molecule Scattering

Computational investigation of low-energy electron scattering from molecules
using UKRmol+ and ASTRA.
