### _Ab Initio_ Electron-Molecule Scattering Calculations for Methane Using UKRMol+ and ASTRA
**Dr. Thomas C. Allison and Dr. Siddhartha Chattopadhyay · National Institute of Standards and Technology**  
*May 2026 – August 2026*

I investigated electron–molecule scattering using _ab initio_ methods for laser and semiconductor etching applications.

I performed scattering calculations with **UKRMol+** and **ASTRA** and systematically evaluated the effects of target-state polarization, electron correlation, basis sets, and active-space selection. I generated correlated target wavefunctions using **Molpro, PSI4, and LUCIA** and developed Python workflows to automate data processing, benchmark computational results against published literature, and generate publication-quality visualizations.

This work involved reproducing and benchmarking published scattering results and developing computational tools to efficiently analyze large scattering datasets.

### Investigating the Defect Behavior and Electronic Properties of Formamidinium Lead Bromide Perovskite through Machine Learned Interatomic Potentials
**Dr. Neeraj Rai · Mississippi State University**  
*May 2025 – August 2025*

I investigated the defect behavior and electronic properties of bulk
formamidinium lead bromide perovskites for semiconductor applications.

I trained machine-learning models on **ab initio molecular dynamics** data to
predict the behavior of bulk crystals containing point defects. I developed
an iterative workflow for model training, performance analysis, data
collection, and retraining to reduce prediction error.

This work resulted in a poster presentation at the **2025 Undergraduate
Research Symposium** at Mississippi State University.

### Fundamental Study of Germanium Nanowire Growth and Deposition
**Dr. Stephen Maldonado · University of Michigan**  
*May 2024 – August 2024*

I studied germanium nanowire growth using an **electrochemical
liquid-liquid-solid (ec-LLS) crystal growth mechanism**, with the broader goal
of understanding materials systems relevant to battery energy storage.

I also developed procedures for fabricating micropipettes, nanopipettes,
microelectrodes, and ultramicroelectrodes to improve experimental efficiency.

This work resulted in poster presentations at the **2024 Undergraduate
Research and Networking at UMich Symposium** and the **2024 AIChE Annual Student Poster
Competition**.
